## Looking Back at a Year of Progress and Innovation

As the year wraps up, it's worth taking a moment to reflect on how much the payments and ATM industry has evolved. From regulatory updates to new technology adoption, this year brought measurable progress — and some important turning points.

Here are the biggest themes we saw across ATM operations, merchant services, and self-service technology.

---

## Innovation Accelerated Across the Industry

The pace of innovation felt different this year — noticeably faster.
Businesses adopted automation tools, self-service kiosks expanded beyond food service, and cash recyclers gained traction in traditional retail and emerging sectors.

### Key innovation highlights:

- Kiosks added new capabilities, including bill pay, verification, and multi-service workflows.
- ATMs saw broader adoption of cash recyclers, reducing labor and armored costs.
- POS systems became smarter, integrating loyalty, inventory, and omnichannel data more seamlessly.
- Monitoring tools like Watchdog gained visibility, as operators prioritized uptime.

The industry didn't simply modernize — it accelerated.

---

## Compliance Became a Major Focus

2024–2025 brought a significant tightening of card-brand requirements.
The most notable was MasterCard's emphasis on address accuracy.

Clear Choice responded early by updating TMS to automatically validate city/state/ZIP combinations against USPS records — ensuring operators stay compliant and portfolios stay clean.

This was one of many reminders that compliance is no longer a background task. It is a performance metric, and acquirers are being evaluated more closely than ever.

---

## Cash Remained Surprisingly Resilient

Despite predictions of a "cashless" world, usage held strong, especially in:

- Convenience & fuel
- Nightlife & hospitality
- Event venues
- Dispensaries
- Rural markets

And with inflation still influencing consumer behavior, cash continues to play a vital role in budgeting and accessibility.

ATMs are far from going out of style — they remain a profitable, stable foundation for independent operators.

---

## Operators Adapted — and Thrived

From small deployers to full-scale portfolios, operators embraced:

- Smarter terminals
- Better data hygiene
- Routing efficiencies
- More consistent monitoring
- Diversification across ATM, POS, and kiosk solutions

This year wasn't just about keeping up — many operators built stronger, more resilient businesses.

---
