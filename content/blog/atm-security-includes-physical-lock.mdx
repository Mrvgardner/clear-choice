When ATM security is discussed, the focus almost always turns to digital threats.

Network breaches.
Malware.
Skimming.
Unauthorized access attempts.

All important.

But there's another vulnerability that often gets overlooked.

The lock.

⸻

## The Industry's Quiet Vulnerability

Most ATMs use standardized, generic lock systems.

In many cases, the same key type can open machines across multiple operators and locations.

Uniform hardware creates uniform exposure.

And while digital monitoring may alert you after something happens, physical access is often the first step in larger incidents.

⸻

## Security Must Be Layered

Digital monitoring is critical.
But digital monitoring alone isn't complete protection.

True ATM security now requires:
- Proactive digital monitoring
- Real-time alerts
- Clean transaction logging
- And physical access protection

Without physical control, digital safeguards are incomplete.

⸻

## How Watchdog Now Covers Both

Watchdog was built to give operators visibility and early insight into network performance and risk.

Now, with the addition of a custom physical lock solution, protection extends beyond the network.

This custom lock:
- Is not generic
- Is not widely shared
- Is intentionally difficult to pick or duplicate
- Reduces predictable physical access risk

Security should not rely on standardized keys.

It should be specific, intentional, and layered.

⸻

## Protection Is About Reducing Opportunity

Security isn't just about reacting to incidents.
It's about reducing the opportunity for them to happen.

Digital monitoring identifies issues early.
Physical security prevents access from becoming an issue at all.

That combination is where modern ATM protection lives.

Payments Without Limits.
Security without shortcuts.
