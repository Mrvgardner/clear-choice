MasterCard recently introduced stricter rules around address accuracy for ATM terminal locations — and many operators across the industry are now scrambling to understand what it means for their business.

Good news: Clear Choice partners don't have to scramble at all.

We've already rolled out a new enhancement inside TMS that ensures your terminal addresses stay aligned with USPS standards and fully compliant with MasterCard's updated evaluation criteria.

Here's what changed, why it matters, and how this upgrade protects your portfolio.

---

## What's Changing — and Why It Matters

MasterCard has tightened its requirements around terminal address accuracy. They are now reviewing acquirers (like Switch Commerce) to make sure the city, state, and ZIP code on file match official USPS records.

Inaccurate or mismatched addresses can lead to:

- **Address integrity findings**
- **Compliance flags**
- **Potential fines**
- **Extra back-and-forth work for partners**

This industry shift makes clean, consistent location data more important than ever.

To stay ahead of the change — and to keep things simple for our partners — Clear Choice and Switch Commerce have already updated TMS to enforce proper address validation.

---

## What You'll See Inside TMS

When you add or edit a terminal location in TMS, the system now automatically checks your city, state, and ZIP code combination.

If the information doesn't match USPS data, you'll get a clear validation message such as:

> "Invalid city/state/ZIP combination."

Just correct the information, align it with USPS records, and save again.

Most partners won't notice any difference unless an address was previously incorrect or incomplete — which makes this a friction-free improvement for the entire network.
