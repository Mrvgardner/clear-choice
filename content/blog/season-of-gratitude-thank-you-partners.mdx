## Pausing to Say Thank You

This time of year naturally encourages reflection — not just on what we accomplished, but on the people who helped make it possible.
And whether you celebrate Christmas, Hanukkah, another tradition, or simply enjoy a quiet winter season, this is the perfect moment to pause and say:

Thank you.

---

## To Our Partners

Your trust fuels everything we do.
Every terminal deployed, every new merchant supported, every portfolio milestone — none of it happens without you.

You push us to innovate, to improve, and to build tools that actually make your business easier. For that, we're sincerely grateful.

---

## To the Operators in the Field

You hustle.
You troubleshoot.
You adapt.
And you keep communities connected, whether through cash access, service availability, or self-service technology that supports local businesses.

Your work matters.
We see it — and we appreciate it deeply.

## To Our Team

Clear Choice and Switch Commerce are built on people who care.
People who monitor networks long after business hours.
People who answer partner questions with patience and clarity.
People who constantly look for ways to make our systems stronger.

The commitment behind the scenes is one of our greatest strengths.

## Looking Ahead With Gratitude

As we move into the new year, we're grateful for:

- The partnerships we've grown
- The challenges we overcame together
- The innovation still ahead
- The opportunity to support you in 2026 and beyond

No matter how you mark the season, we hope it brings rest, connection, and something to look forward to.

From all of us at Clear Choice Payment Solutions,
thank you — for your trust, your collaboration, and your partnership.

Here's to a bright new year.
